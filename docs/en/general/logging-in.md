!!! Note
    Due to security reasons, if you log in with your email address (with a one-time code or a password), you are automatically logged out of the platform after 33 hours. You can log in again in the same way.

You can log in to the 121 platform using the link provided via email together with the credentials. This link is specific to your organization and should be used each time you log in to the 121 platform.

You can log in with a **one-time code** that is sent to your email, or with your **password**.

### How to log in with a one-time code

- Go to the login page.
- Enter your email address in the "E-mail" box. *Make sure you write your email address without any additional spaces.*
- Click the **Send code** button.
- Check your email for a message with the subject "Your 121 Portal sign-in code". *If you can't find it, check your spam folder.*
- Enter the 6-character code in the "Enter your code" boxes. *You can also copy and paste the code. Capital and small letters both work.*
- Click the **Log in** button.

If you entered the wrong email address, click **Go back to the previous page** to change it.

!!! Note "About the one-time code"
    - The code is valid for **10 minutes** and can only be used once. The login page shows how much time is left.
    - If the code has expired or you didn't receive it, click **Request new code**. You can request a new code once every minute. Only the most recent code works.
    - After 5 wrong attempts, the code is blocked. You can request a new code after 15 minutes.
    - If you don't receive a code, check that you used the email address of your 121 account. For security reasons, the login page does not tell you whether an account exists for an email address.

---

### How to log in with a password

If you are **logging in for the first time** you will have received your login details in your email. **Don't forget to change your password the first time you login**. For more details on changing your password see [this page](../general/change-current-password.md).

- Go to the login page.
- Click **Log in with password instead**.
- Enter your email address in the "E-mail" box. *Make sure you write your email address without any additional spaces.*
- **Enter the password that was shared in the email** in the "Password" box. *If you want to check if you've written your password correctly, you can click the eye-icon.:material-eye:*
- Click the **Log in** button.

To go back to logging in with a code, click **Log in with a one-time code instead**.

### Single Sign-On (SSO)

If Single Sign-On (SSO) is enabled in your organization’s system, users will be required to log in using their **Microsoft account email address**. This will automatically log them into the 121 platform. To access SSO, your organization must have enabled it, and your email must be part of the registry.

SSO users cannot log in with a one-time code.

---

!!! Question "Haven't received your login credentials?"
    You can ask a user with Organisational Admin rights in your organisation to verify if an invitation was sent to your email and if your user account was created in the 121 platform within the [**Users** page.](../users/add-users.md)

    If your account has not been set up yet, a user with Organisational Admin rights can send you an invitation by [following the instructions on the Add Users page.](../users/add-users.md)
