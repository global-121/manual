This page explains how to manage the Visa debit card of a registration (pause, unpause, replace, link or close a card) and how to order new debit cards for your program.

!!! Important "Only for programs with Visa debit cards"
    This page only applies to programs that pay through the **Visa debit card** FSP (Intersolve Visa).

    - The **Debit cards** tab of a registration only appears when the registration receives its payments through the **Visa debit card** FSP.
    - The **Debit Cards** tab in **Monitoring** only appears when the program has the **Visa debit card** FSP and your role has the permission to see card orders.

!!! Important "Who can perform actions on this page?"
    - View the **Debit cards** tab of a registration: CVA Manager, CVA Officer, Finance Manager, Finance Officer, View and View without PII.
    - Pause a card: CVA Manager, CVA Officer, Finance Manager and Finance Officer.
    - Unpause a card: CVA Manager, Finance Manager and Finance Officer.
    - Link, replace or close a card, and see or order cards in **Monitoring**: none of the standard roles. These actions need specific permissions that the 121 support team can add to a role when your program needs them.

    [Learn more about Roles & Permissions](../users/description-roles.md)

## Open the debit card of a registration

- Go to the **Registrations** page.
- Click on the registration.
- Click on the **Debit cards** tab.

The tab shows the current card of the registration: **Serial number**, **Card status**, **Current balance**, the amount spent this month, **Issued on** and **Last used**. If there is more information about the card status, it is shown under **Explanation**. Earlier cards of the registration are listed below the current card, each one under **Old card:**.

!!! Note "The buttons depend on the card status"
    The portal only shows the actions that are possible for the current card. For example, **Unpause card** only appears when the card is paused, and **Close card** does not appear for a card that is already closed or expired. **Pause card**, **Unpause card**, **Replace card** and **Close card** also only appear if your role allows the action.

---

## Pause or unpause a card

To pause a card:

- Open the **Debit cards** tab of the registration.
- Click **Pause card**.
- Check the card number in the pop-up window and click **Pause card**.
- A message is sent to the registration about this update.

To unpause a paused card:

- Open the **Debit cards** tab of the registration.
- Click **Unpause card**.
- Check the card number in the pop-up window and click **Unpause card**.
- A message is sent to the registration about this update.

---

## Replace a card

How you replace a card depends on the **Card distribution by mail** setting of the **Visa debit card** FSP of your program.

If cards are sent by mail:

- Open the **Debit cards** tab of the registration.
- Click **Replace card**.
- Read the pop-up window: the current card will be blocked and a new card will be issued.
- Click **Request new card**.
- A message is sent to the registration about this update.

If cards are handed over on site:

- Open the **Debit cards** tab of the registration.
- Click **Replace card**. The **Replace visa card** pop-up window opens.
- Scan the barcode on the back of the new card you hand over to the registration, or type the 19-digit **Serial number** found below the barcode.
- Click **Replace card**.

---

## Link a card to a registration

You can only link a card when cards are handed over on site (the **Card distribution by mail** setting is off) and the registration has no debit card yet. The tab then shows **No debit cards found**.

- Open the **Debit cards** tab of the registration.
- Click **Link visa card**.
- Scan the barcode on the back of the card you hand over to the registration, or type the 19-digit **Serial number** found below the barcode.
- Click **Link card**.

!!! Note "Card number not accepted"
    If the card number is not found, or is already linked to another registration, the portal shows an error. Check the number, or use a different card and inform your supervisor.

---

## Close a card

Closing a card is permanent. The funds on the card are booked back and the card is not replaced.

- Open the **Debit cards** tab of the registration.
- Click **Close card**.
- Read the warning in the pop-up window. This action cannot be undone.
- Click **Close card**.

Closed cards are listed in the **Refunded debit cards** export on the Payments page. See [Payment reporting](./payment-reporting.md).

---

## Order debit cards

You can order new debit cards for your program from the **Monitoring** page. The cards are sent to the address you fill in, for example your branch office.

- Go to **Monitoring**.
- Click on the **Debit Cards** tab.
  *The table under **Cards ordered** shows the orders that were sent to Intersolve successfully. It does not show whether the cards have been delivered.*
- Click **Order cards**.
- Fill in the form:
  - **Number of cards** (from 1 to 700)
  - **Addressee (name of the recipient of the package)**
  - **Addressee email**
  - **Addressee phone number**
  - **City**, **Postal code**, **Street** and **House number**
  - **Addition** (optional)
- Click **Order debit cards**.
- The order is added to the table. Check the **Order status** column to follow its progress.
