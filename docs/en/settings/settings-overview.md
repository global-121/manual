!!! Important "Who can perform actions on this page ?"
    Program Admin can perform actions in all sections. CVA Manager can edit the program information and view the program team. Program Team Manager can manage the program team. [Learn more about Roles & Permissions](../users/description-roles.md)

The **Settings** page lets you review and, if necessary, update your program. There are 5 sections:

- [Program information](../settings/program-information.md): basic details define your program's setup and structure.
- [FSP integration](../settings/fsp-integration.md): FSP(s) integrated with your program, including the manual import/export option with Excel.
- [Registration data](../settings/registration-data.md): registration form integrated to the program, a list of required fields for 121 and your FSP(s), and the fields used to find duplicate registrations.
- [Program team](../settings/programteam.md), under **Users**: users in your program team with their assigned roles.
- [Payment approval](../settings/payment-approval.md), under **Users**: approval process for program payments.
