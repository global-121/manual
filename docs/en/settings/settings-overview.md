The **Settings** page lets you review and, if necessary, update your program. There are 5 sections:

- [Program information](../settings/program-information.md): basic details define your program's setup and structure.
- [FSP configuration](../settings/fsp-integration.md): FSP(s) integrated with your program, including the manual import/export option with Excel.
- [Registration data](../settings/registration-data.md): registration form integrated to the program and a list of required fields for 121 and your FSP(s).
- [Program team](../settings/programteam.md): users in your program team with their assigned roles.
- [Payment approval](../settings/payment-approval.md): approval process for program payments.
